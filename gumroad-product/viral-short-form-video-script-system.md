# The 60-Video Content Engine

## Product promise

Generate a month of short-form video ideas for any niche without staring at a blank page.

Replace every bracketed field before recording:

- `[NICHE]` — topic or market
- `[AUDIENCE]` — specific viewer
- `[PAIN]` — urgent problem
- `[RESULT]` — desired outcome
- `[PROOF]` — evidence, example, or result
- `[OFFER]` — product, service, newsletter, or next step

## Quick-start workflow

1. Choose a niche and audience.
2. Pick one template from each category.
3. Replace the bracketed fields with audience language.
4. Add one concrete example, visual, or proof point.
5. Record a 20–45 second version.
6. Publish, measure retention and saves, then reuse the winning angle.

## Recording card

- Hook: Say the first sentence before introducing yourself.
- Retention beats: change the visual or example every 2–4 seconds.
- Visual: show the problem, the process, and the result.
- CTA: ask for one action only: save, comment, follow, download, or buy.
- Proof: use a real example; label estimates and opinions clearly.
