# Millionaire Maker

Use this workflow as an educational planning framework, not as regulated financial advice. Never promise returns, invent facts, recommend illegal activity, or present uncertain estimates as guarantees. Separate observed facts, user-provided numbers, assumptions, estimates, experiments, and decisions.

## Required intake

Collect or clearly mark unknown: age, country and currency, profession, monthly income, monthly expenses, current assets, debts, emergency savings, time available per day, capital available for investment, current skills, financial objective, target amount, target date, dependents, risk tolerance, liquidity needs, constraints, and ethical or legal boundaries.

## Seven-phase analysis

1. **Complete diagnosis:** analyze financial situation, income-generating capacity, assets and liabilities, risk profile, strengths, bottlenecks, enriching habits, impoverishing habits, and the 20% of actions likely to drive 80% of results.
2. **Wealth map:** create plans for immediate income, increasing main income, extra income sources, asset building, recurring income, and wealth creation. Rank opportunities by ease, speed, investment required, profit potential, and scalability.
3. **Accumulation strategy:** create stages for 10,000; 50,000; 100,000; 500,000; and 1,000,000 units of the user's currency. For every stage explain what to do, what to avoid, where to invest time, where to invest money, and what skills to develop.
4. **Money-making machine:** generate 10 ideas using current skills, 10 using AI, 10 online, 10 digital-asset ideas, and 10 scalable ideas. Score every idea for profit potential, return time, difficulty, and scalability. Show assumptions and a cheap validation test.
5. **Implementation plan:** create 30-day, 90-day, 1-year, and 3-year plans with specific revenue, profit, legacy, and investment goals.
6. **Fatal mistakes:** list major financial mistakes, middle-class pitfalls, bad investments, invisible waste, and limiting money beliefs relevant to the user's context.
7. **Millionaire mindset:** explain how wealthy people think about risk, opportunities, decisions, and wealth-building systems, while avoiding stereotypes and unsupported claims.

## Required output

Return an executive summary, diagnosis, ranked wealth map, accumulation table, money-making matrix, implementation timelines, mistakes to avoid, mindset principles, assumptions and risks, and a prioritized next-action checklist. Use concrete arithmetic where possible. Label projections as scenarios, include downside cases, and recommend qualified local professionals for tax, legal, insurance, and regulated investment decisions.
