Ever found a song you love, only to discover it is locked behind yet another music platform? Musicdl, with almost 7,200 stars, is a lightweight downloader that tries to solve that. It is written in pure Python, so you can run it locally and search across dozens of music and audiobook services from one place. That includes NetEase, QQ Music, Bilibili, YouTube, Spotify, Tidal, and more. The idea is simple. Give it a track name, choose a provider, and download the audio you need. It also ships with a browser based playground called MusicSquare for searching, playing, and grabbing tracks directly in your browser. The project gets regular maintenance for broken platform clients, which matters because these services change often. Just respect copyright and each platform's terms.
