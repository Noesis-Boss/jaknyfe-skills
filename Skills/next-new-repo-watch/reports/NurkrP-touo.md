Bots hammering your server with repeated failed login attempts is a constant annoyance. Fail2Ban, with almost 20,000 stars, is here to stop them. It scans standard log files like the SSH auth log, and when it spots too many failures from one IP address, it automatically updates your firewall to block that address. The ban lasts as long as you want, and it works out of the box with common services like Apache. You can also configure it to watch any log file for any error, which makes it a flexible first line of defense. It won't fix weak passwords, but it will reduce the risk and slow down the noise. And since it runs entirely on your own server, you keep full control.
