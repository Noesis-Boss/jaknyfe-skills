OpenGym is a free, open-source gym and body-weight tracker with no subscription, no account required, and no telemetry. Every time you start with a new gym or trainer, you get told to use yet another app, and your workout history gets scattered. OpenGym keeps your data on your own device, so you own it and share it only with the people you choose. You can plan routines, log workouts, see which muscles you’ve trained, and import from Strong, Hevy, and FitNotes. It’s available as an Android APK and, per the project, in the App Store for iPhone and Apple Watch, and you can also self-host it. If you’d rather not hand all of your health data to a big tech company, this is a simple alternative.
