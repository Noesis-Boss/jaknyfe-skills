AI-YouTube-Shorts-Generator is a free, open-source tool that turns long-form videos, like client meetings, into vertical shorts. It uses an LLM to detect highlights, Whisper to transcribe, and automatic cropping to fit 9:16, with no watermarks and no per-clip credits. It’s an open-source alternative to Opus Clip, Vidyo.ai, Klap, and SubMagic. The big idea from the video is that the whole process can be automated: record your meetings with a tool like Grain, Zoom, or Meet, let the repo find the great moments, and turn them into Shorts, Reels, and TikToks. The approach is simple: post a lot, because a few clips will take off. It’s a great fit for people who do well with text on LinkedIn and X but make no video, and for people who make long-form videos but haven’t started on shorts yet.
