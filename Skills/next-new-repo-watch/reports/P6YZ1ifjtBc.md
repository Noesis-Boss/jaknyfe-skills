You have probably tried to fine tune a language model so it follows instructions, only to watch it miss the mark on being helpful and safe. The Alignment Handbook, with almost 5,800 stars, is a set of robust recipes for that. It walks you through continued pretraining and aligning models with human and AI preferences. Instead of piecing together scattered scripts, you get practical examples for supervised fine tuning and preference based training. The handbook explains why adding preference data can improve helpfulness and safety beyond instruction tuning alone. It connects you to models, datasets, and a technical report so you can reproduce results and adapt the recipes to your project. If you are building a chatbot or tuning an open model, this is a useful reference to keep nearby.
