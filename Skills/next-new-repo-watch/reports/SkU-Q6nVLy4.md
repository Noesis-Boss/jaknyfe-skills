Ever needed a bastion host that doesn't require installing anything on your client machines? Warpgate with almost 8,000 stars is a fully transparent SSH, HTTPS, Kubernetes, and database bastion that works with your existing tools. Just set it up in your DMZ, add users and assign them to specific hosts and URLs inside your network. It records every session for live viewing and replay through a built-in admin web UI. Browser-based SSH, RDP and VNC access is built right in while native clients continue to work seamlessly. It also includes native two-factor authentication and SSO support plus built-in brute force protection. It's a single binary with no dependencies written in safe Rust.
