With all the hosted AI agents out there, people forget how good Hermes Agent is. It’s a free, open-source, self-improving AI agent from Nous Research. It runs on your own computer or server with a beautiful desktop app, works through Telegram, Discord, Signal and iMessage, transcribes voice messages, and learns from experience over time.

Here’s the business idea: lots of businesses can’t use externally hosted AI tools, and they don’t want the hassle of setting up an agent themselves. You install and manage Hermes for them as an AI assistant, a virtual CMO or a lead-generation agent. The key is picking the right vertical, like accounting or law firms: sophisticated enough to need an AI agent, but not so technical that they’d set it up themselves.
