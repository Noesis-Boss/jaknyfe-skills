Ever opened a CRM only to realize you still have to do all the actual busy work yourself? Comp AI CRM with almost 12,000 stars, is an open source CRM built the other way around, where the agent isn't a feature but the core of everything. Most CRMs just give you a database with a form in front of it, and the AI ones bolt a chat box onto the side. This runs on its own deployment with its own work queue. The agent decides what to look at next, books follow-ups, spends a research budget, and stops when the budget runs out.
