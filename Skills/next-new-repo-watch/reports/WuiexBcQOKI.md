Ever wanted to run a whole fleet of coding agents without paying per seat for each one? Orca, with almost 150,000 stars, lets you spin up bots on your own subscription. You bring your own Claude Code or Codex, your own local or remote machines, and your own tools. Then you just ask a bot in Slack, Linear, or GitHub to fix a bug, and come back to a tested pull request. Each bot works in its own workspace, and when it needs your input, it is one tab away. You can watch it in chat or drop into the raw command line and carry the conversation across without losing context. Big tasks get split into worker agents that report back. Honestly, it is a calm way to scale.
