Do you keep digging through scattered pose and outfit references every time you try to direct an AI character? AI Pose Gallery Chinese edition, with almost 10,000 stars, and yes, the content is in Chinese, but I will walk you through it, bundles pose, costume, and portrait references into one static gallery. It includes thousands of clean body poses, depth maps, outfits, and portrait shots, all organized for character design. You can preview everything in the browser, hide sensitive material with the built in safe mode, and connect the library into your image generation workflow. There is also a native ComfyUI custom node plugin, so you can pull references without leaving your setup. No database, no complicated install, just local images and a clean interface.
