Andrew Warner talks with Milind Soni, creator of MausBot, an open-source alternative to GrokBot that gives you multiple AI agents, their own computers, and the freedom to use different AI models.

MausBot goes beyond a simple AI assistant. You can control your own computer, run agents in parallel, schedule automated tasks, connect your team, analyze large datasets, and keep agents running 24/7 in the cloud. Milind demonstrates how it works and explains why open-source AI agents could change how teams get work done.
