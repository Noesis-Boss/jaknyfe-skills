Struggling to port PS5 executables to PC without needing a full emulator? AnyPS5 with nearly 11,000 stars, is a tool that converts PS5 binaries to run natively on both Linux and Windows. It includes a relinker that transforms executables to your target system's format, plus implementations of system libraries for dynamic linking. This means no emulation overhead or separate runtime process is needed. Verified games like Dreaming Sarah run at a stable 60 frames per second even on older hardware. The shader recompiler produces SPIR-V, and unsupported states will throw clear errors. It's a practical approach for developers working on PS5 compatibility.
