Tired of juggling a dozen AI coding assistants and constantly copying tokens between profiles? Cockpit Tools with almost 19,000 stars is here to solve that mess. It is a universal account manager that works with Cursor, Windsurf, Copilot, and many others. You can switch between accounts with a single click and monitor your usage quotas in real time. It even supports running multiple instances of the same tool simultaneously. Honestly, that is a huge time saver for developers who rely on different AI tools for different projects. The interface has full Chinese support and partial English support. Whether you need automatic wake-up features or just cleaner account handling, this tool consolidates everything into one dashboard.
