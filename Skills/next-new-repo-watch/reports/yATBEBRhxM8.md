Looking to cut your software subscriptions without sacrificing powerful content creation tools? Andrew Warner shares seven free and open-source alternatives to popular paid apps for social media scheduling, voice generation, video editing, AI video creation, screen recording, and podcast editing.

The best part? These tools aren't just cheaper alternatives. Many can run on your own computer, work with AI coding agents like Claude Code, and be customized to fit your workflow.

Andrew walks through each tool, demonstrates how his AI agents install and use them, and explains the trade-offs you should know before switching. From scheduling posts across multiple social networks with Postiz to editing video through a transcript with Rescript, these tools offer new ways to create content while reducing recurring costs.

Andrew also explains the limitations of these tools, including self-hosting requirements, hardware constraints, AI editing quirks, and the trade-offs involved in replacing polished paid services with open-source software.

The bigger takeaway: you can use AI coding agents to install software, automate repetitive tasks, customize open-source tools, and build a content creation workflow that works for you.
