Tired of asking one AI model to do everything and watching it struggle? Oh My OpenCode Slim with almost 10,000 stars fixes that by giving you a team of seven specialized agents. Instead of one model doing all the work, this plugin routes each task to the agent best suited for it, like having a scout look at your codebase, a documentation specialist find fresh info, and an architecture reviewer check your design. The orchestrator plans everything out, dispatches specialists in the background, and pulls their results together before moving forward. You can even mix different models for each job to balance quality, speed, and cost.
