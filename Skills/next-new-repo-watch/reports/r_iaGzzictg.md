Newsletter tools like Mailchimp get expensive fast: 50,000 contacts can cost over $300 a month, and the price keeps climbing. Ghost is a free, open-source publishing platform for newsletters, memberships, paid subscriptions and websites, built and constantly improved by its community.

Here’s the business idea: use a tool like BuiltWith to find companies on Mailchimp or similar services. Offer to move them to Ghost, host it for them, and charge a flat fee, say $50 a month for unlimited contacts plus running costs. Bonus: connect it directly to AI agents like Claude or ChatGPT, so customers can have their agent study past newsletters, write new ones and publish them without clicking around a dashboard.
