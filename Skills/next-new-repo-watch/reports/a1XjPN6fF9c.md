Ever wondered where ancient Chinese cities like Jiankang or Chang'an actually sit on today's map? Jiangshan Map, with almost 9,400 stars, lets you scrub through eighteen dynasties from 403 BCE to the present. It contains over 200 territory snapshots, so you can watch imperial borders shift century by century. You can trace a city's genealogy through time, seeing every name it carried across two millennia. Classical poetry routes are included too, so you can follow Su Dongpo's exile to Huangzhou or Han Yu's journey through Languan Pass. And if you read a poem, you can jump directly to the actual place it describes. Honestly, it makes history feel explorable, not just readable.
