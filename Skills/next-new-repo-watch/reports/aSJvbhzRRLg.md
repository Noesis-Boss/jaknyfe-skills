CleanYourTV is a free, open-source tool that cleans the bloatware off any Android or Google TV over Wi-Fi and gives you a clean, Apple TV-style home screen. Smart TVs and streaming dongles come loaded with ads, tracking, and background apps, and even after digging through every settings menu, some of it keeps running with no button to turn it off. CleanYourTV goes deeper: you back up first, switch on debugging in the developer menu, connect, and it removes the junk without rooting your TV. The result is a faster, cleaner home screen, and because everything is reversible, you can undo it if you change your mind. It works on Android TV and Google TV devices, including the small streaming dongles many people use.

Back up first, and only modify devices you own. Disabling system apps can affect some features.
