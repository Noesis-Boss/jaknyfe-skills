Need to share a sensitive file without exposing your identity or location? OnionShare, with nearly 7,200 stars, lets you do exactly that using the Tor network. It creates a temporary onion service, giving you a dot onion URL to share with your recipient. They connect through Tor, and the file transfers directly between your computers. No central server ever sees your data. You can also host an anonymous website or run a private chat room. It works on Windows, macOS, and Linux. The tool is designed to be simple, so you don't need to be a privacy expert to use it safely. Honestly, it's one of the most user-friendly ways to get strong anonymity for file sharing.
