Ever found yourself stuck with proprietary document software that feels slow and locked down? WordCraft, with nearly 10,000 stars, is an open source, clean-room reimplementation of Microsoft Word built entirely in pure Rust. It gives you the familiar Word workflow, including the ribbon, styles, tables, track changes, references, and mail merge, so you can switch without a learning curve. It reads and writes docx files natively, and runs on macOS, Windows, Linux, BSD, and even in the browser through WebAssembly. Because it's built in Rust, it's incredibly fast and reliable, and since it's open source, you can inspect every line of code or fork it for your own projects. If you've ever wanted a modern, lightweight alternative to Word that doesn't compromise on features, this one is worth a look.
