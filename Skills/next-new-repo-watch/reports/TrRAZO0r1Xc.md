Tired of your Chinese input method making you page through endless wrong candidates? 万象拼音, or Wanxiang Pinyin, with almost 6,000 stars, and yes, the content is in Chinese, but I'll translate it for you, aims to fix that. It is a Rime schema built on a deeply optimized dictionary and grammar model. The dictionary includes tone marked Pinyin, phrase structure, and accurate word frequencies, so predictions feel natural and fast. You get full Pinyin, double Pinyin, and auxiliary codes like Xiaohe and Ziranma. Dozens of Lua scripts add a calculator, dynamic timestamps, and a symbol library for thousands of Unicode characters. There are four versions. Base for most users, Pro for heavy double Pinyin fans, Lite for a lighter setup, and Pure for older systems without Lua. It runs locally.
