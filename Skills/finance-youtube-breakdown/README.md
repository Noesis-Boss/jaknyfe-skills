# Finance YouTube Breakdown

Zo skill for validating faceless finance-business YouTube opportunities and producing approval-gated faceless video drafts.

The scheduled automation runs Mondays at 8:00 AM America/Phoenix and writes briefs to `noesis_content/youtube/finance-breakdown/`. After explicit approval and QC, it may publish only to `https://youtube.com/@Noesisgroup`; it does not publish elsewhere, spend money, contact sponsors, or change channel settings.
