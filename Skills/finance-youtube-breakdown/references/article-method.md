# Article method: extracted and corrected

Source: https://freedium-mirror.cfd/https://medium.com/write-a-catalyst/this-boring-finance-niche-is-quietly-paying-new-youtube-channels-5-700-month-86bccefca9d5

The article proposes faceless finance-business breakdown videos: study several competitor transcripts, extract hook and pacing patterns, identify topics competitors have not covered, write original scripts, create scene-by-scene image prompts, optionally animate stills, analyze thumbnails, and prepare titles, descriptions, tags, and AI/educational disclaimers.

Useful parts:

- Evidence-led competitor and gap research.
- A repeatable research → script → visual plan → packaging workflow.
- Consistency as an explicit experiment rather than judging one upload.

Unverified or overstated parts:

- Channel identities, subscriber counts, earnings, and RPM claims were not independently established by the article.
- “Monetized within the first month” and “20–30 uploads to monetization” are not reliable expectations.
- Competitor transcripts and frames may be copyrighted source material; analyze, do not imitate.

Operational correction: use the article as a testable hypothesis. Verify current channel metrics through public evidence or the owner's analytics, cite sources, and use a controlled 8–12-video test.
