| Time | Risk | Mitigation in script |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | Hook drift | Hook states 3 objections + 10-min time cost; promises concrete list of what viewer will have done |

## Pattern check

- **Open loop:** free-vs-paid promise in hook pays off at 9:30 — holds viewers through the dip zone.
- **New visual beat** every ~90 seconds (demo → list → demo → lecture → verdict).
- **Payoff placement:** vocabulary at 3:30 and three uses at 5:30 land before the median drop-off point for tutorial audiences.
- **CTA sequencing:** homework → subscribe → Friday preview; single ask chain, no stacking.

## Fixes applied in correction pass

1. Tightened on-camera intro from ~50s to ~30s of spoken content.
2. Moved paid-tier mention later so the "don't spend a dime" hook isn't contradicted early.
3. Added "if you can write an email" callback at 0:30 — matches hook's skill objection.
