2. "Most AI videos are made by 25-year-olds for 25-year-olds. This one isn't."
3. "You don't need to be technical, you don't need to be young, and you don't need to spend a dime — you just need the next ten minutes."

## Winner — Title: #1, Hook: #3

**"AI for Beginners Over 40: Start Here"** — exact-match search phrase ("AI for beginners"), the age qualifier self-selects the target viewer and removes judgment, and "Start Here" promises an ordered entry point rather than another hot take. It's the channel anchor; every later video can be linked back to it.

**Hook #3** — answers all three objections (skill, age, money) in one breath, states a ten-minute time cost (watch tolerance fits), and creates the open loop: what exactly will these ten minutes buy me? It also sets the no-cost promise the video's final segment pays off.

## Why they'll convert

- Title captures search + suggested traffic from people typing beginner AI queries.
- Age qualifier in the title filters out the wrong audience, boosting early retention (YouTube's strongest signal).
- Hook's "spend a dime" promise sets up a legitimate reason to stay to the end (free-vs-paid explained at minute ~9), which protects mid-video retention.
- Peer positioning ("grown-ups," no guru energy) matches the channel profile differentiator.
