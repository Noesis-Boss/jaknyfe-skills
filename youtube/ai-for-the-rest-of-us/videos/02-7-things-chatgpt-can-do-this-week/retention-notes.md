# Retention Notes — 7 Things ChatGPT Can Do for You This Week

## Retention model
Numbered list = visible progress bar. Viewers who see "number 3 of 7" know exactly how much value remains, which sustains watch-through better than an unstructured demo reel.

## Beat map (MM:SS targets at tested ~240 wpm narration pace)

| Beat | Time | Job |
|------|------|-----|
| Cold open hook | 0:00–0:20 | Promise: 7 things, free, under 5 min each. "Pick two" lowers commitment. |
| Intro credibility | 0:20–0:50 | Don + 40 years + Friday brand. Links to video 1 for binge path. |
| Thing 1 (email tone) | 0:50–2:00 | Fastest universal win. Live typing on screen from second 5 of the segment. |
| Thing 2 (brain-dump → plan) | 2:00–3:15 | Voice-input demo = "I can do this without typing" moment. |
| Thing 3 (summaries + deadline) | 3:15–4:10 | The deadline trick is the mid-video re-hook — teased in cold open implicitly, paid off here. |
| Thing 4 (decode fine print) | 4:10–5:20 | Story beat: $600 lease clause in 10 seconds. Highest-stakes segment. |
| Thing 5 (brainstorm 20) | 5:20–6:20 | Energy lift after two "serious" segments. |
| Thing 6 (proofreader) | 6:20–7:10 | Quick win. |
| Thing 7 (rehearse conversations) | 7:10–8:20 | Surprise payoff — the "wait, it can do THAT?" segment. Placed last-before-outro so the ending cliff is high. |
| Reality check | 8:20–9:10 | Safety brand moment; also catches skipped-ahead viewers. |
| Outro + homework | 9:10–9:40 | "Pick two this week" — concrete, small. Subscribe loop to Tuesday/Friday cadence. |

## Open loops / payoffs
- Cold open promise ("all seven with exact words to type") → every segment shows a literal typed prompt.
- Hook #5 (lease clause story) from titles-hooks.md runs as the Thing 4 payoff.
- "I'll come back to safe use at the end" (Thing 3) → reality check. One loop only; no bait.
- Homework callback to video 1's homework pattern (one small real task) → series habit.

## Risk points
- Segments 3–6 are the dip zone (minutes 3–7). Countermeasures: every segment opens with the demo within 5 seconds, no meta-talk between segments.
- Thing 4 must land the "not legal advice" caveat in under 10 seconds — long disclaimers crater retention.
- Do not let the reality check run past ~50 seconds; it sits between the emotional high (Thing 7) and outro.
