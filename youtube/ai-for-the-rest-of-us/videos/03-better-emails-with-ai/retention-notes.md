# Retention Notes — How to Write Better Emails With AI—Without Sounding Robotic

## Retention model
Open-loop + payoff structure. The cold open promises "four words" (step 2) but withholds them until 2:00, forcing watch-through past the natural drop point. Step 3 (Sent-folder trick) and step 4 (six tells) are listable, screenshot-able value — high save/share likelihood, which YouTube rewards.

## Beat map (MM:SS targets at tested ~240 wpm narration pace, ~7:10 runtime)

| Beat | Time | Job |
|------|------|-----|
| Cold open (recognition hook) | 0:00–0:20 | "I hope this email finds you well" — universal shared enemy. Four-steps/5-minutes/Free contract set immediately. |
| Intro credibility | 0:20–0:55 | Don + 40 years + Tuesday brand. "Mood, not a job" plants the thesis. |
| Step 1 (job not vibe) | 0:55–2:00 | Live typing from second 5. First payoff inside 2 minutes — protects the drop cliff. |
| Step 2 (four-word fix) | 2:00–3:00 | OPEN LOOP CLOSED. "Make it sound human" is the thumbnail/title promise — deliver with visible before/after. |
| Step 3 (sound like you) | 3:00–4:10 | Sent-folder trick — highest save-rate moment. Screen-record the actual paste. |
| Step 4 (six tells) | 4:10–5:10 | Listable value; on-screen text per tell. Screenshot bait. |
| Safety minute | 5:10–5:40 | Trust-builder. Short — do not lecture. |
| Outro + homework | 5:40–6:10 | One email assignment + comment prompt. Tease video 4 (big-3 comparison). |

## Re-hook checkpoints
- **1:45** — after step 1 payoff: "Now, the four words I promised."
- **2:50** — after the human-fix demo: "But there's a problem — it sounds human. It doesn't sound like YOU."
- **4:05** — before the tells: "Even a good draft slips these in. Six of them."

## Retention risks
- **Emails are personal** — viewers may mentally test against THEIR inbox. Mitigation: the six-tells segment is a checklist they apply to their own mail; keep it concrete and rapid.
- **Four steps (not seven)** — less list-progress pressure than video 2. Mitigation: the open-loop on "four words" carries the front half; the six tells act as a second numbered list in the back half.
- **Safety minute drop risk** — keep to 30 seconds, on camera, one take. It converts skeptics; do not cut it.

## Measurement
- Check 30-second retention ≥ 70% (recognition hook should outperform video 1's cold open).
- Check the 2:00 dip: if step 2 under-delivers the open loop, viewers leave. If retention holds through 3:00, the method works as scripted.
- Save rate is the success metric for the six tells — pin the tells as a comment to capture it.
