# The Six AI Tells (Free Download)

Companion to: **How to Write Better Emails With AI—Without Sounding Robotic**

Everything below works on the free version of ChatGPT. Copy and paste the prompts.

## The four steps

### Step 1 — Give it the JOB, not a vibe
"Professional" is a costume, not an instruction. Tell the AI what the email is *for*.

```
[Your draft email]

This email needs to get a meeting scheduled with a busy client
without nagging. Keep it to five sentences. End with a specific
date and time question.
```

### Step 2 — The 4-word fix
If an AI draft sounds off but the information is right, don't rewrite from scratch:

```
Fix the structure only
```

That keeps your words and voice, and fixes the order, flow, and rambling.

### Step 3 — Make it sound like YOU
Open your own Sent folder. Pick the one email you're proud of — the one that sounds like you on your best day. Then:

```
Match this style exactly:

[paste your sent email]

Now rewrite my draft to sound like that.
```

### Step 4 — Run the six-tells check
Paste your final draft and ask:

```
Flag anything in this email that reads as AI-generated filler.
Be specific. Quote the exact phrases.
```

## The six AI tells

1. **The filler opener** — "I hope this email finds you well." You have never once hoped an email finds someone well. Delete it; open with the actual point.
2. **Em-dash stacking** — the tell isn't one em-dash; it's six in the same email. Humans do it for emphasis; AI does it by default.
3. **Delve / leverage / seamless** — three words that almost never appear in real emails and always appear in AI ones.
4. **Bullet lists in threes** — AI loves three bullets even when one sentence would do. If bullets are decorative, cut them.
5. **The summary-that-repeats** — "In summary, as mentioned above…" If the email is short enough to need a summary, it's too long.
6. **Joyless exclamation points** — "Great question!" from software that has never once been delighted.

## The safety minute (from the video)

- **You handle facts, AI handles tone.** Check every name, number, and date yourself.
- **Never paste passwords, account numbers, customer data, or private information** into any chatbot.
- **Read the final version out loud once.** Your ear catches what your eye skims.
