## Most people think it's too late to learn AI. It isn't.

Here's the thing I keep hearing from people my age: *"I missed the boat on this stuff."*

You didn't. You're actually in a better position than the 25-year-olds making most of the AI videos you've seen — you know what a real problem looks like, you know when an answer is nonsense, and you've got forty years of context no chatbot has.

What you don't have is a first step that isn't wrapped in hype.

So I made one.

**"AI for Beginners Over 40: Start Here"** goes live this Tuesday. Ten minutes, no jargon, no sales pitch. By the end you'll have:

- Asked an AI your first real question — not "what is AI," something you actually need this week
- Learned the five words of AI vocabulary that actually matter (it's five, not fifty)
- A straight answer on whether the free version is enough (it is, for most people)

If you can write an email, you can do everything in this video. That's not a slogan, it's the actual bar.

This is the start of a new series on this publication — practical AI help for people with jobs, lives, and forty years of experience that no chatbot can replace. The channel is called **AI for the Rest of Us**, and the video drops Tuesday.

Watch for it. And if there's one AI task you've been putting off because it felt too technical — reply and tell me what it is. That's what the next videos are for.
