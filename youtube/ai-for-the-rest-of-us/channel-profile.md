# Channel Profile — AI for the Rest of Us

*Stages 1–2 complete. Defined by Don, 2026-09-08. Reuse for every video — do not re-derive.*

## Niche

Practical, jargon-free AI guidance for adults 40+ who want to use AI confidently in everyday work, business, and creative projects.

## Positioning statement

> "I help experienced adults understand and use AI without the hype, technical jargon, or feeling that they've fallen behind."

## Differentiator

Age and experience are the advantage — not another young "AI guru," but the knowledgeable peer who tests tools, explains what genuinely works, and connects AI to real professional and creative experience.

## Audience profile (Stage 2)

- **Who**: Experienced adults 40+, often managing work, a small business, or creative projects; feel behind on AI and tired of hype.
- **What they want**: Confidence and practical wins they can use the same day, explained in plain language.
- **Problems**: Jargon overload, fear of looking foolish, scam/privacy worries, tool-choice paralysis, not knowing where to start.
- **What makes them click**: Promises of specific, concrete outcomes ("this week," "without sounding robotic"), peer-level tone, honesty about limits.
- **Watch tolerance**: 8–15 minutes; tutorials must show the screen, not just talk.
- **What makes them subscribe**: Consistent practical value from someone with real professional experience they can trust.

## Content pillars

1. **Everyday AI** — email, research, planning, documents, spreadsheets, organization, decision-making.
2. **AI for small business** — marketing, customer communication, operations, automation, proposals, productivity.
3. **AI for creators** — writing books, publishing, artwork, game development, building fictional worlds.
4. **AI confidence and safety** — avoiding scams, protecting privacy, checking accuracy, understanding limitations, choosing tools without overspending.

Personal interests (history, Freemasonry, Tucson, gaming, books) are examples inside videos — not separate channel topics.

## Publishing format

- Two videos per week: **Tuesday** = focused tutorial solving one problem; **Friday** = tool test, comparison, experiment, or real project.
- 8–15 minutes. Brief on-camera intro, then screen demonstrations with voice-over.

## Monetization order

1. Affiliate links to genuinely demonstrated tools
2. Beginner workshops and individual AI consultations
3. Downloadable prompt guides and practical templates
4. Beginner course or paid membership
5. Sponsorships
6. YouTube advertising
7. Books, games, and related creative products
