# Finance metric definitions

- Gross profit = revenue − cost of goods sold.
- Gross margin = gross profit ÷ revenue; report as unavailable when revenue is zero.
- EBITDA = net profit + taxes + interest + depreciation + amortization when those fields exist; otherwise label the estimate and list missing components.
- Net cash movement = cash received − cash paid, including the selected operating, investing, and financing scope.
- Monthly burn rate = average monthly net cash outflow over the selected lookback; do not call a net inflow a burn.
- Runway = available cash ÷ positive burn rate; report unavailable when burn is zero or negative.
- Budget variance = actual − budget. Also show percentage variance when the budget is nonzero.
- Days overdue = max(0, report date − due date) for unpaid invoices.
- Average collection time = collected invoice days-to-pay weighted by invoice amount when payment dates exist.

Always state whether a metric is actual, inferred, estimated, or forecast.
