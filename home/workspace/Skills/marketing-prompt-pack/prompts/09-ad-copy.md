# Ad Copy

> Prepend the CONTEXT block + grounding preamble from `SKILL.md` to every prompt.

1. Write 5 Facebook ad headlines for [product] targeting [audience] who struggle with [pain point]. Each headline should be under 40 characters and create urgency.
   - ✏️ Patch: urgency must be truthful — no fake deadlines or fake discounts.

2. Write a native ad script for [product] that blends into editorial content. Start with a relatable story, introduce the product naturally, and end with a soft CTA.
   - ⚠️ Patch: paid placement must carry the platform's required sponsorship disclosure. "Blends into editorial" = tone match, not concealment.

3. Create 3 variations of Google ad copy for [product/service]. Include a strong CTA, highlight one key benefit, and address the main objection [objection].

4. Generate 10 ad hooks for [product] using these emotional triggers: fear of missing out, social proof, curiosity, and desire for transformation.
   - ✏️ Patch: social-proof hooks require real proof points from CONTEXT — no invented "10,000 customers" claims.

5. Write a retargeting ad for people who visited [landing page] but didn't buy. Acknowledge their hesitation, restate the key benefit, and offer a reason to return.

6. Create YouTube pre-roll ad copy for [product] that delivers a compelling message in the first 5 seconds before the skip button appears. Keep the full ad under 30 seconds.

7. Write a carousel ad sequence (5 cards) for [product]. Card 1: problem. Card 2-4: one benefit each. Card 5: CTA with offer.

8. Write a video sales letter script under 90 seconds for [product]. Hook, problem, solution, proof, offer, CTA.
   - ✏️ Patch: proof section = real results or [NEED INPUT].

9. Generate 3 split-test ad body copy variations for [product], each testing a different angle: (1) transformation, (2) fear of inaction, (3) social proof.
   - ✏️ Patch: angle 3 requires real testimonials/stats — [NEED INPUT] if none provided.
