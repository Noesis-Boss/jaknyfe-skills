> Prepend the CONTEXT block + grounding preamble from `SKILL.md` to every prompt.

1. Write a sales page for [product] targeting [audience]. Include: attention-grabbing headline, empathy-driven intro, benefit bullets, social proof section, offer breakdown, guarantee, and CTA.

2. Create a VSL (video sales letter) script for [product]. Under 10 minutes. Follow: hook, problem, agitate, solution, proof, offer, bonuses, guarantee, CTA.

3. Write the offer stack copy for [product]. List the core deliverable, 3 bonuses, total value, your price, and a reason why it's priced lower. Make it feel like an obvious yes.

4. Write 5 objection-busting copy blocks for [product] that address: price, time, skepticism, 'not for me', and 'I'll do it later'.

5. Create a checkout page bump copy for a $[price] add-on to [main product]. Write the headline, 3-bullet value summary, and one-click CTA in under 100 words.

6. Write an order confirmation page upsell for [higher-ticket offer]. They just bought [product]. Show them the next logical step using the momentum of their purchase.

7. Create a one-time offer (OTO) script for [product] shown after opt-in. You have 5 minutes of their attention. Include scarcity, value, and a clear yes/no decision.
   - ✏️ Patch: scarcity must be real (real cap or deadline) — invented scarcity is [VERIFY] and gets cut.

8. Write the copy for a pricing page with 3 tiers for [product/service]. Include tier names, what's included at each level, who it's for, and a recommended plan badge.

9. Generate a proposal or pitch email for [service] tailored to a [business type] client. Include the problem you solve, your approach, what's included, investment, and next step.
