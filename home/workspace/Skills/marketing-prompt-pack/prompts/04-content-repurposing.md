# Content Repurposing

> Prepend the CONTEXT block + grounding preamble from `SKILL.md` to every prompt.
> This is the pack's strongest category — every prompt is paste-based, so output is grounded by construction. Keep it that way: never let the model fill gaps from its own knowledge.

1. Turn this blog post into 10 tweets: [paste post]. Each tweet should stand alone, provide value, and end with a hook to the full article.

2. Convert this YouTube transcript into a LinkedIn article: [paste transcript]. Keep the core insights, restructure for written format, and add a strong intro hook.

3. Take this email newsletter and repurpose it into: (1) a short-form video script, (2) an Instagram carousel outline (5 slides), (3) a 280-character tweet. Source: [paste email]

4. Repurpose this podcast episode summary into 3 different content formats for different platforms: a Twitter thread, a LinkedIn post, and a short blog intro. Summary: [paste]

5. Extract 5 standalone social media quotes from this long-form piece. Each quote should be insightful, punchy, and require zero context to understand. Source: [paste]

6. Turn these 3 customer testimonials into a short case study story, a social proof carousel, and a bullet-point FAQ section. Testimonials: [paste]
   - ✏️ Patch: testimonials must be real pasted text — do not polish wording beyond light grammar fixes.

7. Repurpose this sales page into an email nurture sequence of 3 emails: awareness, consideration, decision. Sales page: [paste]

8. Convert this how-to guide into a Reels or TikTok script (under 60 seconds), a carousel post (6 slides), and a Twitter thread. Guide: [paste]

9. Take this webinar Q&A section and turn it into a FAQ blog post, a 5-tweet educational thread, and a short explainer for a landing page. Q&A: [paste]
