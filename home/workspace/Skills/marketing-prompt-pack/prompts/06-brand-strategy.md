1. Define a brand voice for [company] that sells [product/service] to [audience]. Output a voice guide with 4 personality traits, do/don't examples for each, and a sample tagline.

2. Write a brand positioning statement for [company] using this format: For [target audience] who [need/want], [brand] is the [category] that [key benefit] because [reason to believe].
   - ✏️ Patch: "reason to believe" must be a real differentiator from CONTEXT, not a generic claim.

4. Create a brand story for [company] using the hero's journey: who they were before, the turning point, what changed, and the mission they're now on.

5. Write brand messaging pillars for [company]. Generate 4 pillars, each with a one-line statement, 2-sentence explanation, and 3 talking points.

6. Develop a brand promise for [company] that is specific, credible, and memorable. Then write 3 ways this promise would show up in customer experience.
   - ✏️ Patch: promise must be deliverable — flag anything we can't actually fulfill as [VERIFY].

7. Write an ideal customer profile for [brand]. Include demographics, psychographics, biggest fears, core desires, where they spend time online, and what they say when recommending products like ours.
   - ✏️ Patch: ICP must be built from my customer descriptions; invented demographics are [VERIFY].

8. Create a brand messaging matrix for [company] with tailored messages for: cold audience, warm audience, and existing customers.

9. Audit the messaging of [brand] based on this copy: [paste copy]. Identify gaps, inconsistencies, and write 3 improvement recommendations.
