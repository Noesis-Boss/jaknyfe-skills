# Case Studies & Testimonials

> Prepend the CONTEXT block + grounding preamble from `SKILL.md` to every prompt.
> ⚠️ Highest-risk category in the pack: this is where invented proof becomes publishable fabrication. The grounding preamble is not optional here.

1. Write a customer case study for [client/customer] using this structure: situation, complication, solution, results. Use real or placeholder metrics and quotes. Topic: [topic].
   - ✏️ Patch: placeholders must be visibly labeled [PLACEHOLDER] — never shaped to look like real data.

2. Turn this customer testimonial into a full 500-word case study with context, challenges faced, how [product/service] helped, and measurable results. Testimonial: [paste]
   - ✏️ Patch: measurable results must come from my data — [NEED INPUT] if not provided.

3. Create a case study landing page for [client story]. Include headline with result, 3-section story (before/during/after), a pull quote, stats bar, and CTA.
   - ✏️ Patch: stats bar requires real numbers from CONTEXT; no invented figures.

4. Write a mini case study (150 words) formatted for a sales email insert. Focus on one specific result, one quote, and one CTA.
   - ✏️ Patch: same rule — real result + real quote or [NEED INPUT].

5. Generate 3 hypothetical case study scenarios for [product] showing different use cases for: [persona 1], [persona 2], [persona 3]. Include a before/after format.
   - ⚠️ Hard patch: INTERNAL BRAINSTORMING ONLY. Output must be labeled "Illustrative example — not a real customer" if it ever leaves the doc. Never publish as proof.

6. Write a case study for a B2B client that focuses on ROI. Include: client background, challenge, solution implemented, timeline, and 3 specific results with numbers.
   - ✏️ Patch: the 3 results require real client data — [NEED INPUT] for each missing number.

7. Create a social proof story post (case study in story format) for Instagram or LinkedIn about a customer who achieved [result] using [product/service].
   - ✏️ Patch: customer and result must be real; composite/fictional customers are banned in published posts.

8. Write the questions you would ask a customer to extract a compelling case study. Generate 15 interview questions that uncover the emotional journey and hard metrics.

9. Turn this data/result summary into a compelling case study narrative that tells a human story while backing it up with numbers. Data: [paste results or metrics]
