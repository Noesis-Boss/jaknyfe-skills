# Capture Friction or Lose Everything (Ch. 3)

## The three-tap threshold (the rule that decides if a capture system survives)

If adding a thought takes **more than three taps, it does not happen.** Three taps is a literal count of physical interactions from idea → exists outside your skull (unlock, open, record). Your brain runs a cost-benefit analysis on every micro-task; when capture cost exceeds the thought's perceived value (always low, in the moment), the brain drops it.

**Anti-pattern:** tools that make you decide where a note belongs at capture time (notebook, tag, title) — that's a retrieval question asked at capture time. Capture first. Process later. Two different clocks.

## Preserve the heat (hot state vs cold state)

Ideas arrive **hot** — wrapped in context, emphasis, the why. A 15-second voice memo preserves more usable signal than a three-line typed note, because speech is 3-4× faster than typing and carries texture. Six hours later, the cold note ("Offer tiered pricing for Marek") loses the reasoning that made it right.

- **Voice is the primary capture mode** — not a fallback for typing.
- The screenshot IS the thought — don't describe what you can capture.

## The three capture lanes (all drain into ONE flat bucket)

| Lane | Owns | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **Voice** | In-motion moments (walking, driving, shower) — where diffuse-mode ideas arrive | One-tap lock-screen widget → transcription → substrate. Speak full sentences; the context is worth 2 seconds. |
| **Text** | Desk moments (reading, on calls, scanning email) | Global hotkey → two sentences → same bucket. |
| **Visual** | Image-shaped thoughts (wireframes, pricing pages, layouts) | Screenshot → same bucket, searchable. ~1/3 of useful consultant signal is not text-shaped. |

Principle: **always use the fastest lane for the current mode.** No folders, no filing at capture. Organize once a week (~20 min) when switching cost is chosen, not forced. Skip a week and the bucket deepens, retrieval cost climbs, and the stack dies.

## The 30-second re-articulation test

Can you re-articulate a captured thought in 30 seconds — without re-listening, re-reading fully, opening a nested folder? **If not, it was stored, not captured.** Force the pipeline to produce a clean title/first line per memo. Retrieval must be a glance.
