# find-moneymaking-biz

## Overview

Generate a short, evidence-aware opportunity shortlist. Treat income targets as hypotheses, not promises.

## Intake

Collect or label assumptions for: skills, available hours, startup budget, existing audience or buyer access, preferred delivery mode, ethical or industry constraints, target income, and deadline.

## Process

1. Generate 5–10 ideas tied to a painful buyer problem, existing workaround, reachable channel, and specific paid deliverable.
2. Score each idea from 1–5 on skill fit, buyer access, pain urgency, speed to first sale, delivery simplicity, and competition risk. Explain scores briefly.
3. Reject ideas that require unverified demand, large upfront spend, regulated claims, proprietary access, or unrealistic volume.
4. Choose one recommendation and state the strongest reason it could fail.
5. Design a 48-hour test: target buyer, offer, outreach or landing-page action, evidence threshold, stop rule, and maximum time or spend.

## Output

- Assumptions
- Opportunity table with score, rationale, risks, and first paid deliverable
- One recommended idea
- Three failure reasons
- 48-hour validation test
- `PROCEED`, `ITERATE`, or `STOP` gate

Do not claim profitability, market size, demand, or conversion without evidence. Route a surviving idea to `customer-validation` before substantial building.

## Usage

### Evals

Use `evals/evals.json` when available. Check that outputs distinguish assumptions from evidence, include a bounded test, and avoid guaranteed-income language.
