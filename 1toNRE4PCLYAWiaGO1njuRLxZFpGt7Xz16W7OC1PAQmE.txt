Date: Friday, October 23rd.

Food: Available for separate purchase through El Zaribah Shrine.

One of the highlights of the evening will be Tables of Terror, a friendly competition where participating Masonic organizations will transform a table into their own unique Halloween-themed display or activity. This is an opportunity for your members to showcase their creativity, interact with others, and compete for having the best Tables of Terror entry.
We respectfully ask that you share this invitation with the members of your [local chapters/bodies] and encourage them to attend and participate in the Tables of Terror.

* All decorations must be securely fastened and should not create trip hazards or block walkways.

* Decorate within your assigned space; do not encroach on neighboring tables.
* Arrive early to complete your display before the party begins; do not dismantle your decor until the event ends.[a][b][c]
* Plan to clean up all decor materials, props, and trash before leaving your site.
* Optional: Add themed games or activities.

Thank you for your consideration and for everything you do to support the young men of Arizona DeMolay. We look forward to welcoming you and your members on October 23rd.
Once again, we look forward to strengthening the Arizona Masonic Family, one scare at a time.

Haunted Tour
Good day, [Title],
I am pleased to write to you on behalf of the Arizona DeMolay State Association to formally invite [Shrine Club] to join us for our annual Halloween Party on October 23rd, held in conjunction with El Zaribah Shrine’s Fourth Friday program.
Building upon last year’s mission of strengthening the Arizona Masonic Family, we hope to once again bring together members of our Masonic organizations for an evening of fellowship, fun, and community. This year’s event will also support the El Zaribah Shrine General Fund, helping the Shrine continue its work serving those in need. Last year, our Halloween Party raised $1,100 for the Shrine, and our goal this year is to build upon that success and raise substantially more.
Event Information
Date: October 23rd.
Admission: $10 per person.
Time: 5:00 PM - 9:30 PM
Location: El Zaribah Shrine (552 N 40th St, Phoenix Arizona, 85008).
Food: Available for separate purchase through El Zaribah Shrine.
Admission will include access to all of the evening’s activities, including a costume contest, dancing, games, and other Halloween festivities.
The Haunted Tour
One of the major attractions of this year’s Halloween Party will be our Haunted Tour. Rather than creating a traditional haunted house in one location, we hope to transform part of the Shrine building into a walk-through Halloween experience, with guests traveling down the hallways and through the rooms decorated by participating Shrine Clubs.
We are asking each Shrine Club to help bring the Haunted Tour to life by decorating your shrine room with a Halloween-themed display. Each club will have the opportunity to create its own unique atmosphere, whether that means a spooky and eerie scene, a fun Halloween display, or something entirely different.
Live actors are completely optional. Clubs are welcome to simply create an impressively decorated room, or, if they wish, add members in costume to interact with guests and make their room even more immersive.
The goal is for guests to walk from room to room and experience something different at every stop, with the combined efforts of the Shrine Clubs creating one large Haunted Tour throughout the building.
Haunted Tour Guidelines
To help keep the Haunted Tour safe and enjoyable for everyone, we ask participating clubs to follow these guidelines:
* All decorations must be securely fastened and must not create trip hazards or block walkways.
* Use battery-operated lights rather than candles or open flames.
* Avoid electrical cords or other materials across walkways where guests could trip.
* Keep all themes appropriate for a family-friendly event. Scary and spooky themes are encouraged, but please avoid excessively gory, frightening, or potentially offensive content.
* Please be mindful of materials that may cause allergic reactions, including strong scents or airborne materials.
* Remain within your assigned room or area and do not obstruct hallways or neighboring spaces.
* If using live actors, please ensure that their activities remain safe and appropriate for guests of all ages.
* Clubs are welcome to incorporate games, interactive elements, sound effects, or other activities into their room.
* Please have your room set up before the party date, to ensure that all is ready for the fun. 
* Decorations and props should remain in place until the conclusion of the event.
* Each club is responsible for cleaning up its decorations, props, and trash before leaving.
We respectfully ask that you share this invitation with the members of your Shrine Club and encourage them to attend and participate in the Haunted Tour. If your club is interested in decorating a room, please let us know when registering so that we can coordinate the tour and ensure that guests have the best experience possible!
Our hope is that this event will be more than just a Halloween celebration. It is an opportunity for the Arizona Masonic Family to come together, build new relationships, support the Shrine, and create an experience that our members, families, and guests will remember.
Thank you for your consideration and for everything you do to support the young men of Arizona DeMolay. We look forward to welcoming you and your members on October 23rd and seeing what you bring to the Haunted Tour.
Once again, we look forward to strengthening the Arizona Masonic Family, one scare at a time.
Fraternally,
State Master Councilor
Bro. John Enloe
