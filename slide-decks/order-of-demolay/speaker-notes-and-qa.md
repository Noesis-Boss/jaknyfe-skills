# The Order of DeMolay — Speaker Notes

## 1. The Order of DeMolay
Welcome everyone. This is a quick look at what DeMolay feels like and how it can help a young man grow.

## 2. What is DeMolay?
DeMolay is a youth leadership organization built around friendship, service, character, and responsibility. It is something members experience together.

## 3. You do more than attend meetings
The point is participation: young members lead, serve, build friendships, and grow through real responsibilities.

## 4. Seven virtues. One direction.
The virtues are a compass, not a claim that anyone is perfect. DeMolay gives members a community in which to practice them.

## 5. Your chapter is youth-led
Young members plan and lead. Adults provide the structure, guidance, training, and safety support that make youth leadership possible.

## 6. What might you actually do?
The exact calendar differs by chapter, but the pattern is consistent: service, leadership, social connection, and meaningful traditions.

## 7. Skills that travel with you
Connect the examples to school, work, teams, interviews, and future leadership. The value is practice, repeated over time.

## 8. Who can join?
State the general DeMolay International range, then immediately defer to the local chapter for current jurisdictional details and requirements.

## 9. You do not have to arrive ready
Lower the pressure. A prospective member can visit, try an activity, ask questions, and decide with good information.

## 10. Come see what you can become
End with a concrete invitation: talk to the local chapter, attend an event, and bring questions or a friend.

## Tough audience questions

1. **Is DeMolay a religious organization?**  It includes reverence for God and asks members to respect faith, morality, and conscience; local chapters can explain how meetings and ceremonies are conducted.
2. **How much time does it take?**  It depends on the member and chapter. Members can start by attending selected activities and choose a sustainable level of participation.
3. **What does it cost?**  Fees vary by jurisdiction and chapter. Ask the local chapter for current amounts and what they cover.
4. **Is it safe?**  Adult advisors support chapter activities under DeMolay’s youth-protection requirements. Families should ask the local chapter about supervision, transportation, and event policies.
5. **What if I am shy or do not know anyone?**  That is a normal reason to visit. A chapter should assign someone to welcome a guest and explain what is happening.
