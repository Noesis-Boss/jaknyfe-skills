# KDP defaults for future books

Use these as starting defaults, not automatic final decisions; confirm book-specific exceptions before submission.

- Primary marketplace: Amazon.com (United States)
- Territories: Worldwide, when rights are owned
- Kindle Select: Enroll initially
- Kindle DRM: Disabled
- Kindle price: $6.99 regular; consider $1.99 launch promotion
- Paperback: Yes, Amazon KDP print-on-demand
- Paperback trim: 6 × 9 inches
- Interior: Black ink on cream paper, no bleed
- Cover: Matte
- Paperback ISBN: Free KDP-provided ISBN
- Paperback price: $17.99 starting point
- Expanded Distribution: Enabled
- Hardcover: Defer for initial launch
- Release: As soon as KDP approves
- Audience: Adult/general readers; no children's reading-age range
- AI disclosure: Manuscript text is not AI-generated when AI is used only for research/editing/proofreading/formatting; disclose AI-generated cover art separately
- Rights: Confirm ownership of manuscript, quotations, case studies, images, and included material
- Examples and unsupported figures: Label illustrative composites and illustrative estimates with assumptions unless sourced
- Paperback interior quality bar: Mirrored print margins, at least 0.5-inch gutter for 274+ pages, safe outer/top/bottom margins, EPUBCheck, Calibre reader-proof, and visual PDF checks

These defaults came from the 2026-08-21 KDP setup for *Outsource Your Own Brain*. KDP rules, pricing, royalty bands, and available options may change; verify current guidance during each upload.
