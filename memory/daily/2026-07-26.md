# Story Arc Candidate — 2026-07-26

## Title
Diary of a Corpse Who Forgot They Were Dead

## Focus idea
Prevents character inconsistency because a dead person has no stakes, so every action is inherently paradoxical and the voice stays fixed by the contradiction itself.
