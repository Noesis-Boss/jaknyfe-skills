# Coordinated KDP Launch Plan

**Decision:** Build one connected author business with two clearly separated author identities.

## Author architecture

| Book | Public author | Positioning | Amazon author page |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Outsource Your Own Brain* | **D.E. Lowery** | Practical AI and cognitive-work systems for solopreneurs | Separate D.E. Lowery page |
| *Bound by Ash and Thorn* | **Jonathan Thorne** | Fantasy romance; Book One of *The Withered Court* | Separate Jonathan Thorne page |

Use Don Lowery's legal identity only in KDP account, tax, payment, and identity-verification fields. Enter D.E. Lowery or Jonathan Thorne only in the book's public author field. Do not combine the books on one Amazon author page or cross-link the pen name to the real-name brand unless deliberately disclosed later.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Launch *Outsource Your Own Brain* first.** It is shorter, practical, and can establish the real-name author platform, email list, and content engine.
2. **Launch *Bound by Ash and Thorn* 4–6 weeks later.** Use the interval to build the Jonathan Thorne identity, collect early fantasy-romance readers, and finish print proofing without rushing the novel.
3. **Keep the business connection behind the scenes.** One publishing/admin workflow, two public brands, separate landing pages, newsletters, reader magnets, social profiles, and Amazon Author Central pages.

## Format plan

### D.E. Lowery — *Outsource Your Own Brain*

- Kindle eBook: launch-day edition.
- Paperback: launch-day edition after physical proof approval.
- Optional hardcover later; do not delay launch for it.
- Lead magnet: one useful worksheet or chapter companion, delivered through a D.E. Lowery email list.

### Jonathan Thorne — *Bound by Ash and Thorn*

- Kindle eBook: launch-day edition, with an eBook pre-order only after the final manuscript and cover are locked.
- Paperback: launch-day edition after proof approval.
- Series metadata: *The Withered Court*, Book One.
- Back matter: reader invitation for Book Two and a Jonathan Thorne reader-list signup.

## 8-week operating calendar

### Weeks 1–2 — Prepare

- Lock titles, subtitles, author names, descriptions, covers, trim sizes, categories, and seven keyword phrases per format.
- Proof both current PDFs and EPUBs on multiple screen sizes; inspect cover, front matter, table of contents, chapter starts, hyperlinks, and back matter.
- Create separate author bios, landing pages, email lists, and social identities.
- Set up or confirm KDP legal/account information under Don Lowery.

### Weeks 3–4 — Launch D.E. Lowery

- Upload eBook and paperback as matching editions.
- Order and inspect a paperback proof.
- Publish a short sequence of useful posts, excerpts, and examples from the book.
- Ask launch readers for honest reviews after they have read the book; never incentivize or script reviews.
- Track sales, pages read, conversion, reviews, and the search terms producing traffic.

### Weeks 5–8 — Launch Jonathan Thorne

- Open the Jonathan Thorne reader list with a short atmospheric reader magnet or sample chapter.
- Release cover reveal, premise, character/world material, and a clean sample chapter.
- Upload the final eBook and paperback; use eBook pre-order only if the final files are genuinely frozen.
- Order and inspect the print proof.
- Run a concentrated launch week, then shift to steady series-building content.

## Metadata direction

### *Outsource Your Own Brain*

**Core promise:** Help AI-curious solopreneurs delegate repetitive cognitive labor while keeping judgment, identity, and responsibility.

**Keyword themes:** AI for solopreneurs; cognitive offloading; personal AI assistant; productivity systems; knowledge work; business automation; decision making.

**Category direction:** business productivity, small business/entrepreneurship, and technology/AI categories that are available and accurately describe the final book.

### *Bound by Ash and Thorn*

**Core promise:** A mortal cartographer who can sense lies is drawn into a dangerous court where maps, magic, and trust are inseparable.

**Keyword themes:** fantasy romance; romantasy; enemies to lovers; magic court; forbidden magic; morally gray fantasy; slow burn fantasy romance.

**Category direction:** fantasy romance, romantic fantasy, and epic/high fantasy categories that accurately fit the finished novel.

## Non-negotiable safeguards

- Keep **D.E. Lowery** and **Jonathan Thorne** consistent across cover, title page, copyright page, metadata, author bio, and retailer listings.
- Correct the stale Bound README author line before using it as a publishing reference; the current title page, copyright, and back matter establish Jonathan Thorne as canonical.
- Do not put a legal name, private email, or home address in public book metadata.
- Do not publish either title until the exact author name and metadata are final; KDP treats author-name changes after publication as a new-edition problem except for limited typo corrections.
- Use accurate categories and keywords. Do not stuff metadata with other authors, bestseller claims, or unrelated search terms.

## Success criteria

- Both books have clean Kindle and paperback editions.
- Each book has the correct public author identity everywhere.
- Each author has a separate Amazon Author Central presence and reader funnel.
- The books launch sequentially without exposing or merging the two identities.
- First 30 days produce a baseline for conversion, reviews, traffic sources, and follow-up marketing.
