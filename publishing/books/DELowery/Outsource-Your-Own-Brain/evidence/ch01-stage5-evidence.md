## Summary

**A. Peer-Reviewed Studies (5 verified)**
- **Cowan (2001)** — *Behavioral and Brain Sciences* 24(1). The "~4 chunks" capacity. Foundation citation for working-memory ceiling.
- **Leroy (2009)** + **Hood/Leroy/Schmidt (2016)** — *OBHDP* 109(2) & 137. Attention residue: incomplete task switching taxes the next task.
- **Risko & Gilbert (2016)** — *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* 20(9). Defines "cognitive offloading" as adaptive, not weakness. The chapter's conceptual anchor.
- **Adler & Benbunan-Fich (2012, 2015)** — *Int. J. Human-Computer Studies* 70(2) & *Interacting with Computers* 27(4). Switching between **dissimilar** tasks carries the steepest penalty — directly supports the 3-load model collapse.
- **Sparrow, Liu & Wegner (2011)** — *Science* 333(6043). The brain already offloads when external storage exists; facts exit, pointers stay. Frames offloading as evolved, not degenerate.

**B. Statistics (3 verified)**
- **US Census Bureau NES 2023** (released May 15, 2025): 30.4M nonemployer establishments, $1.8T revenue. Demographic scale of the solopreneur condition.
- **Cross, Rebele & Grant (2016)** — *HBR* 94(1): ~80% of work time in meetings/email/communication. Coordination overload number.
- **Microsoft Work Trend Index (June 2025)**: 31,000 workers, 31 markets; interrupted every ~2 minutes, 275×/day. Death-of-deep-work number.
