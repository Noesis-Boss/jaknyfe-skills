# About the Author

D.E. Lowery is a technology executive, entrepreneur, database engineer, and full-stack software developer with more than three decades of experience helping organizations leverage technology to solve complex business challenges. Throughout his career, he has successfully bridged the gap between executive leadership and technical innovation, designing solutions that improve operational efficiency, streamline processes, and create lasting organizational value.

Don earned a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration (BSBA) in Management Information Systems from the University of Arizona, where he developed the foundation that would shape his career in technology and business systems. He later completed his Master of Business Administration (MBA) at the University of Phoenix, further strengthening his expertise in strategic leadership, operations, finance, and organizational management.

His career began during the formative years of the commercial Internet, serving as Director of Operations for Slip.Net, one of Arizona's pioneering Internet service providers. As the dot-com era accelerated, he joined Epylon.com as Senior Director of Operations, where he helped scale enterprise operations, technology infrastructure, and business systems for one of the early leaders in online commerce. These experiences provided a unique perspective on the rapid evolution of Internet technologies and the operational disciplines required to support high-growth organizations.

Today, Don serves as Managing Partner of Noesis Management Group, where he advises businesses on digital transformation, enterprise architecture, systems integration, custom software development, database engineering, artificial intelligence, and business process optimization. His consulting philosophy emphasizes practical innovation—aligning technology with strategic objectives to deliver measurable business outcomes rather than technology for its own sake.

Over the course of his career, Don has architected enterprise databases, designed cloud-based applications, developed full-stack web solutions, implemented business intelligence platforms, automated complex workflows, and led multidisciplinary teams through transformational technology initiatives. His ability to understand both the technical architecture and the executive decision-making process has made him a trusted advisor to organizations seeking sustainable growth through technology.

Beyond consulting, Don is an accomplished designer, author, and creative technologist whose personal projects span tabletop game design, educational software, artificial intelligence, digital publishing, and interactive media. His work reflects a lifelong passion for combining technology, creativity, and storytelling to educate, inspire, and build communities.

Whether leading enterprise technology initiatives, mentoring development teams, or designing innovative products, Don believes that the best technology is ultimately about people. His guiding philosophy remains simple: clarity over complexity, strategy before implementation, and technology that creates lasting value.
