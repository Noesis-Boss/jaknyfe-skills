**Subject:** The chapter that almost didn't make it

Not because it was wrong — because it was the same advice as everyone else's: pick your tools, write better prompts, automate more. The book didn't earn its existence until it said something the tool-chasers don't: some of your work should never be automated, and a real system has to say which.
