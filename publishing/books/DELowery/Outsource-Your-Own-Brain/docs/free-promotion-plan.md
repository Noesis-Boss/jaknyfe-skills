# Free Promotion Plan — Outsource Your Own Brain

Zero-direct-cost plan. Amazon Ads begin only after sales revenue funds them. All recommendations use only claims in `source_library/outsource-your-own-brain.yaml` — no invented stats, reviews, or rankings.

## 1. Amazon listing optimization (free, do once)

- **Keywords (7 slots):** use phrases buyers type, not author name or ISBN:
  1. AI productivity for solopreneurs
  2. delegating work to AI
  3. small business automation book
  4. AI workflow systems
  5. ChatGPT for business owners
  6. solopreneur time management
  7. AI tools for consultants
- **Categories:** choose three in KDP — Business Software / AI & Machine Learning / Entrepreneurship (pick the three with lowest best-seller ranks that still match the book).
- **Description:** rewrite in direct-response order: problem (drowning in AI busywork) → promise (keep judgment, delegate the rest) → proof (chapter map = deliverables) → call to action (scroll up, get Kindle or paperback). No fabricated results, no review quotes.
- **Author Central:** claim the profile, add bio + headshot, link the blog/substack feed — free surface Amazon displays on the listing.
- **Editorial review section:** paste your own Substack excerpt as an "About the author" editorial note via Author Central (allowed; don't invent third-party blurbs).

## 2. KDP Select promotional windows (free, schedule)

Book is already enrolled in Kindle Unlimited. Sequence, roughly one move per month:

1. **Free Days (5 days/90-day period):** run 3 consecutive free days, announced 10–14 days ahead across X, LinkedIn, Substack, and the M/W/F post slots. Download volume lifts the sales-rank signal after pricing returns.
2. **Countdown Deal (remaining period):** 7 days at $0.99 with the US/UK banner, announced as "limited time" — true scarcity, permitted.
3. Never combine Free Days and Countdown in the same 90-day window.
4. Track each window's rank and KENP reads in `publishing/books/DELowery/Outsource-Your-Own-Brain/MEMORY.md` to learn which move earns more.

## 3. Review flywheel (free, rule-bound)

- Add a "If this helped, please leave an honest review on Amazon" line to: the book's final page (needs a rebuild), Substack footer, and every Free/Countdown announcement.
- Never ask for a *positive* review, never exchange reviews, never post on behalf of others. One-line honesty ask only.

## 4. Owned channels (free, already running)

- X: 1 post/day as @jak_nyfe (automation `OYYB Book Posting Lane`), max 2 promotional posts per 7 days.
- LinkedIn: 3 posts/week (M/W/F) as D.E. Lowery.
- Substack: weekly newsletter issue derived from the book's pillars; every issue ends with the book link.
- zo.pub: reader sample of the intro chapter stays public at https://zo.pub/jaknyfe/outsource-your-own-brain as the no-gate preview.
- Pinterest (later): repin quote cards from chapter takeaways — free account, search-traffic longevity.

## 5. What NOT to do

- No buying reviews, no review swaps, no fabricated testimonials (source library `never_publish`).
- No direct EPUB/PDF download links while KDP Select is active — Amazon exclusivity violation.
- No invented scarcity or deadlines.
- No bulk unsolicited DMs.
