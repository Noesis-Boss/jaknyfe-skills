# Paperback wrap cover design

## Goal

Create a KDP paperback cover PDF for *Outsource Your Own Brain* using the assigned paperback ISBN `9798194009725`.

## Fixed production inputs

- Trim: 6 × 9 inches
- Interior: 274 pages, black ink on cream paper, no bleed
- Spine estimate: 0.685 inches for 274 cream-paper pages
- Full cover with bleed: 12.935 × 9.25 inches
- Finish: matte

## Layout

- Preserve the supplied front-cover artwork on the right panel.
- Use a matching cream/navy/teal treatment on the back and spine.
- Put the existing book description on the back cover.
- Put the title and author on the spine.
- Reserve a white lower-right back-cover area for the KDP barcode.
- Do not include a cover page in the interior PDF.

## Verification

- Confirm PDF page size and single-page cover format.
- Render and visually inspect the full wrap, front art, spine alignment, back-cover text, and barcode area.
- Do not publish or submit the KDP title.
