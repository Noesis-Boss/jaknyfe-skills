Riddles
25 Halloween Riddles of Varying Difficulty
Easy Riddles
   1. Riddle: I’m orange and round, carved with a face. On Halloween night, I light up the place. What am I?
Answer: A jack-o’-lantern.
   2. Riddle: I fly on a broom and wear a black hat. I cackle and cast spells, now who is that? What am I?
Answer: A witch.
   3. Riddle: I’m a spooky spirit, floating in the air. I might give a chill, but I’m not really there. What am I?
Answer: A ghost.
   4. Riddle: I’m wrapped up tight in strips of old cloth. I stumble from tombs, no words from my mouth. What am I?
Answer: A mummy.
   5. Riddle: I’m a creepy crawler with eight legs to spare. I spin sticky webs to catch prey unaware. What am I?
Answer: A spider.
Medium Riddles
      6. Riddle: I howl at the moon when it’s full and bright. My claws and my fur make a fearsome sight. What am I?
Answer: A werewolf.
      7. Riddle: I’m a house with creaks, where shadows abide. On Halloween, brave souls dare step inside. What am I?
Answer: A haunted house.
      8. Riddle: I’m a creature of night, with wings soft and sly. I hang upside down where the shadows lie. What am I?
Answer: A bat.
      9. Riddle: I bubble and brew in a cauldron’s deep. Witches add newt’s eye to make their spell steep. What am I?
Answer: A potion.
      10. Riddle: I’m not alive, yet I stand in the field. With straw in my gut, I make birds yield. What am I?
Answer: A scarecrow.
Hard Riddles
         11. Riddle: My thirst is eternal, I shun the day’s light. With fangs I partake, in the neck I bite. What am I?
Answer: A vampire.
         12. Riddle: I rattle and clank, no flesh on my frame. Under moonlight I dance, by magic’s dark claim. What am I?
Answer: A skeleton.
         13. Riddle: I wail in the night, my cry spells your doom. A spirit of sorrow, I haunt by the moon. What am I?
Answer: A banshee.
         14. Riddle: I’m a steed from the dark, with eyes like hot coal. I gallop through nightmares, no rider controls. What am I?
Answer: A nightmare (mythical horse).
         15. Riddle: In a circle of stone, I’m called by old rites. My presence brings madness, I lurk in the night. What am I?
Answer: A demon.
Very Hard Riddles
            16. Riddle: I’m a dealmaker old, with a contract of flame. For your heart’s desire, your soul I’ll claim. What am I?
Answer: The Devil.
            17. Riddle: I’m a headless rider, my steed swift and grim. With a lantern I hunt, when the moonlight is dim. What am I?
Answer: The Dullahan.
            18. Riddle: I’m a relic of woe, with a curse that won’t fade. My mirror shows nothing, yet fear is displayed. What am I?
Answer: A cursed locket.
            19. Riddle: I lead a grim march when the veil is most thin. With specters and hounds, I hunt souls lost to sin. What am I?
Answer: The Wild Hunt.
            20. Riddle: I’m a shadow of frost, in the harvest moon’s glow. I steal breath from the living where cold winds do blow. What am I?
Answer: A wendigo.
Expert Riddles
               21. Riddle: I’m a Celtic dread, with a face not my own. I weave through the mists, where no light has shone. My name’s tied to tricks, and I’m rarely alone. What am I?
Answer: A pooka.
               22. Riddle: I’m a spectral maid, bound to a well’s deep despair. My voice lures the curious, but none leave my lair. What am I?
Answer: A rusalka.
               23. Riddle: I’m a flame in the dark, but no warmth do I give. I lead fools to their doom, where no man should live. What am I?
Answer: A will-o’-the-wisp.
               24. Riddle: I’m a guardian of graves, with a form carved in stone. I weep for the lost, but I’m never alone. My gaze turns to stone those who meet it at night. What am I?
Answer: A weeping angel (inspired by folklore-like statues, not strictly Doctor Who).
               25. Riddle: I’m a ritual’s end, born where blood meets the earth. My name’s lost to time, yet I herald rebirth. In Samhain’s old fires, I’m the hunger that wakes. What am I?
Answer: The Horned God.
