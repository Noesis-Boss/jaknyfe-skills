About AMFC

For nearly 30 years, we've equipped Arizona educators to reach children most in need — and we're growing to bring hope to more communities.

Our story

A nonprofit built to help Arizona's at-risk youth thrive

The Arizona Masonic Foundation for Children is dedicated to helping Arizona's at-risk youth thrive. Our flagship educator training program equips teachers with the skills and resources to recognize challenges early and provide the support children need to succeed.

As we grow, we're committed to developing additional child-centered programs that strengthen schools, empower teachers, and create brighter futures for students across the state.
